Privacy policy

What this site collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version

Chainwise is a static publication. Reading a guide does not require an account, and the site does not ask for your name unless you send a message through the contact form.

Advertising is delivered by third party networks. Those networks may set cookies or read device identifiers in order to serve and measure an ad. That part is outside our direct control, and the choices you make in your browser or with the network apply to it.

What is collected

Server logs. The hosting provider records the request, the time, the requested page, an approximate location derived from the IP address, and the browser’s user agent. These logs keep the site online and are used for security and traffic totals.

Messages you send. If you use the contact form, the name, email, subject and message you enter are sent to the desk. They are used to reply and are kept only as long as the correspondence needs them.

Advertising data. Ad networks may collect a cookie identifier, IP address, approximate location, browser and device details, and the pages where an ad was shown. They use this to select an ad, cap frequency and report results.

Cookies

Chainwise itself does not set a login cookie. Third party ads and their measurement partners may set cookies. You can block third party cookies in your browser settings, and you can clear them at any time. Blocking them does not stop the guides from working.

Where the law requires consent for advertising cookies, it is collected by the ad network or its consent tool before those cookies are set.

Analytics

If an analytics package is added, this page will name the provider and what it measures before the change goes live. Aggregate counts from server logs are used in the meantime.

Legal bases

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, server logs are processed on the basis of legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and available. Contact messages are processed to answer your request. Advertising cookies rely on consent where consent is required, and on the network’s own legal basis elsewhere.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data the desk holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest, and you can complain to your national data protection authority.

Requests go through the contact page. Because the desk keeps very little, most requests will end with a confirmation that no record exists.

Retention and sharing

Server logs are kept for a short operational window and then discarded. Contact messages are kept while the correspondence is active. Data is shared with the hosting provider and with ad networks as described above. It is not sold.

Children

The site is written for adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone under sixteen.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change that affects what is collected will be described on this page before it takes effect.

Contact

Privacy questions and data requests go through the contact page.
