Contact the desk

Corrections first. Tips, data and advertising enquiries are read in that order.

The desk reads every message

What to send

A correction needs three things: the sentence, the source that contradicts it, and the date you checked. A tip needs a document or a link that can be verified. An advertising enquiry needs the placement, the creative size and the dates.

Please do not send seed phrases, private keys, exchange passwords or identity documents. The desk does not need them, and a message is not a secure channel.

If you are locked out of an exchange or a wallet, contact that provider from its own help page. Never through a link or a message you did not start yourself.

Advertising

Display placements are sold by third party networks and appear on every page, labelled Advertisement. Chainwise does not sell editorial coverage, paid rankings or links inside a guide.

Security

The desk will never ask for a seed phrase, remote access to your device, or a payment before a reply. Treat any message that does as an attack, whoever it appears to come from.
