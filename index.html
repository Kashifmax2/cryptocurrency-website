The lead story

By the Chainwise desk, 9 November 2026

A blockchain is a ledger that nobody owns. Many computers keep the same history and agree on the order before writing.

No bank sits in the middle, and no single company can edit the past alone.

Each block carries the fingerprint of the one before it. Change an old entry and every later block breaks.

Rewriting a payment means redoing the work above it, faster than the rest of the network.

That cost is the design. A payment is final when reversing it costs more than the payment.

Confirmations are a price, not a ritual. The full guide takes the block apart.
